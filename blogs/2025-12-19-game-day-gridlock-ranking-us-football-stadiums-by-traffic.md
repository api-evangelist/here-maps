America’s pro football league is the most-watched sports competition in the United States, drawing millions of fans on each game day. As the 2025 season comes to an end, HERE Technologies analyzed traffic patterns around all 30 major stadiums to identify which venues experience the most severe game day congestion—making it harder for fans to get to the game.
