As electric vehicles (EVs) move from early adoption into the mainstream, a survey conducted as part of the HERE-SBD EV Index 2026 found that 27% of internal combustion engine (ICE) vehicle owners are much more likely to consider an EV than they were a year ago. Even so, only 17% identify an EV as the vehicle type they are most likely to purchase or lease next.
